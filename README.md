# Claws of Courage

A fast-paced action combat game built with p5.js. Play as a tiger fighting waves of monsters in an endless survival challenge.

## Features

- **Combat System**: Light and heavy attacks with combo system
- **Progressive Difficulty**: Waves get harder with more enemies, health, and damage
- **Boss Battles**: Face powerful bosses every 5 waves
- **Weapon Upgrades**: Get stronger weapons every 5 waves
- **Shop System**: Upgrade damage, speed, health, and attack speed between waves
- **Endless Waves**: The game continues indefinitely with increasing difficulty
- **Power-ups**: Collect health, speed, and damage boosts during combat

## Controls

- **Arrow Keys / WASD**: Move
- **Up Arrow / W**: Jump
- **Z / K**: Light Attack
- **X / L**: Heavy Attack
- **SPACE / ENTER**: Start game, Continue from shop/rewards
- **R**: Return to menu from game over

## How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in a web browser
2. Press SPACE to start
3. Fight monsters and survive as long as you can
4. Between waves, spend coins on upgrades
5. Every 5 waves, face a powerful boss and get a weapon upgrade

## Game Mechanics

- Monsters spawn from both sides and move toward you
- Health regenerates slowly over time
- Boss waves occur at waves 5, 10, 15, 20, etc.
- Weapon levels increase your base damage multiplier
- Monsters are larger than the tiger and pose a real threat

Good luck, and may your claws strike true!
